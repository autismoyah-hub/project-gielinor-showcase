## Capture classes

**Live native gameplay** means the actual native client raster in an isolated running Rust client/server session. Automated ordinary actions were used in many development runs; these images do not establish human first-play pacing.

**Prepared native render** means model/scene submission to the Rust rendering path without a live world simulation, UI or active actors unless the caption explicitly says otherwise. It does not establish gameplay integration. The anvil reference/candidate panel retains its original matched composition.

**Native animation preview** means an authored asset/action played by the native animation/render harness. It does not establish combat, pathfinding or gameplay integration. The complete GIFs retain their original bytes and timing.

**Blender render/diagnostic** uses Blender lighting or a clay construction view. It is not native game appearance.

**AI concept illustration** is a generated design reference, not a screenshot, reconstructed geometry, or proof of completed implementation.

## Publication suitability

Only selected rendered images and original animation previews are distributed. Source cache files, extracted models/animations, private JVM reference material, editable source packages, account records and credentials are excluded. Source identifiers in the image manifest are report names and source filenames, not user-specific absolute paths. Captured player handles are isolated development/test accounts. Jagex reference imagery and authentic game assets are credited, with no claim to their ownership or license.

The early A/B/C offline studies had previously been retained locally pending publication review. The present assignment explicitly requests publishing historical layout evidence to this showcase repository. Only their rendered screenshots are included, with attribution and concept status; their source cache and scene packages are not redistributed.

## Verification scope

The deliberately small BBCode subset is parsed with strict nesting and known attributes; all 72 image tags resolve to source-verified images at an immutable raw GitHub commit. The three previews are generated from the exact BBCode. Original source images and final contact sheets were visually reviewed. All PNG pixel buffers and dimensions match their sources; all GIFs retain original bytes, decoded frames, frame durations and loop settings.

Browser inspection of the local previews was blocked by the browser file-URL policy. That policy was not bypassed. Structural validation and image inspection were completed independently. No claim is made that an authenticated Rune-Server editor, its theme, image proxy or final submitted reply has been tested. The provided HTML previews are a portable approximation, not a screenshot of Rune-Server.
