The Cinderworks: The Broken Water Return Hi everyone! Here is a look at how Floor One has developed, from early layout experiments into an enclosed industrial complex with its own furniture, machinery and architectural families. The images are development studies and live test captures; the final art direction is still under review.

Finding the shape of the floor The earlier playable map established the preparation and guardian routes. We then explored a sanctuary-led layout, a waterway and exploration layout, and a more direct foundry progression. All three remain concept studies; none was approved for production.

From open courts to a connected interior The direction moved toward an enclosed shell: connected rooms, supported thresholds and a water return through the middle. The concept illustration below is a visual target, followed by actual native-rendered geometry.

Giving each working space a purpose These matched prepared workshop views show the furnishing and paving pass. The later layout replaces the old rail arrangement with fixed lifting equipment, sorting surfaces, manual trays and a trackless handcart.
