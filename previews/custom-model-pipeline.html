Blender to the native Rust client — custom asset experiments Hi everyone! We have also been experimenting with an AI-assisted authoring workbench for original RuneScape-inspired 3D assets. The useful test is how a model looks and moves in the native Rust client, so Blender beauty renders and native results are kept separate. The first conversion proof An early procedural arch went from an editable Blender scene into the native model format, then into an isolated live world. The lighting changes between the Blender and native images are real; conversion does not carry Blender’s whole material system with it.

Authentic-asset studies also exposed failures. The Fire Giant club-variant test reached a static comparison and a separate corrected motion retest after the first motion attempt failed; it does not establish a finished original NPC or independently authored choreography. Convincing organic creatures and consistent high artistic fidelity remain active research problems. The pipeline is increasingly useful for structures and machinery. We are still learning how to make creature shapes, faces, materials and motion feel authored rather than procedural. Thanks for looking — criticism of the native results is especially welcome. Only review images and original animation previews are included here. Authentic anvil imagery is credited to Jagex; no cache meshes or extracted reference animations are distributed.
